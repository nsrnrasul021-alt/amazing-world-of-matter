01 • THE BIG IDEA

What is matter?

Look around — matter is everywhere.
🔎

Matter is anything that takes up space and has mass.

In simple words: matter is all around us. A book, a toy, water and even air are matter.

📚

BOOK

It takes up space.

🧸

TOY

It has mass.

💧

WATER

It takes up space.

🌬️

AIR

It has mass too!

02 • MEET THE THREE

Three states. Three behaviors.

🧊01

SOLID

“I keep my own shape.”

  • Fixed shape
  • Fixed volume
  • Does not flow
EXAMPLESIcePencilBookChair
💧02

LIQUID

“I can flow and fit my container.”

  • No fixed shape
  • Fixed volume
  • Can flow
EXAMPLESWaterMilkJuiceOil
💨03

GAS

“I spread out and fill space.”

  • No fixed shape
  • No fixed volume
  • Spreads out
EXAMPLESAirWater vaporOxygenCooking gas

04 • QUICK SORT

Matter or not matter?

Guess, then open a card to check. Matter has mass and takes up space.

🪨A pebbleCHECK

Yes! It has mass and takes up space, so it is matter.

🎈Air in a balloonCHECK

Yes! Air is made of gases. It has mass and fills space.

🔦A beam of lightCHECK

No. Light carries energy, but it is not matter.

🔊A soundCHECK

No. Sound is energy traveling through matter, but sound itself is not matter.
